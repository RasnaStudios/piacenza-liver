About the Piacenza Liver

The Piacenza Liver is an ancient Etruscan bronze artifact dating to approximately 100 BCE, used for hepatoscopy (liver divination). It contains 42 inscriptions representing various Etruscan deities organized into cosmological zones.

Key Information

The liver contains 42 inscriptions representing 25 Etruscan deities organized into 9 cosmological zones: Sky, Water, Earth, Underworld, Pars Familiaris, Gall Bladder, Central Section, Pars Hostilis, and Back/Bottom.

Major deities include Tinia (Jupiter), Nethuns (Neptune), Turan (Venus), Laran (Ares), Hercle (Hercules), Usil (Sun), and Tiur (Moon).
